# PRD: Relational Layers

**Status:** Ready to build as a bounded prototype  
**State in SpiritTree:** Inside the organism, with constraints  
**Version:** v1  
**Product type:** Relational discernment tool  
**Initial territory:** Friendship and emerging romance

## Thesis

Relational Layers helps a person name where a relationship actually is, decide whether they want it to deepen, and identify the healthiest next move: deepen, hold, clarify, or step back.

The product should function as a consent-forward discernment system that produces cleaner conversations, clearer pacing, and clearer boundaries.

## Why SpiritTree Should Build It

- It widens agency by helping users make better relational decisions.
- It strengthens sovereignty by countering confusion, projection, and passive drift.
- It can serve the commons if built as relational literacy infrastructure rather than emotional extraction.
- It compounds if the framework becomes a durable language users can reuse across contexts.

## Why This Is Dangerous

This category is full of products that feel profound while quietly becoming manipulative, pseudo-clinical, or emotionally irresponsible.

The main risks are:

- fake authority
- overconfident interpretation
- implied diagnosis
- pressure toward intimacy as if depth were inherently good

v1 needs clear scope, restrained claims, and outputs that change behavior.

## Product Position

Relational Layers is:

- a structured reflection and discernment tool
- a pacing and consent aid
- a generator of concrete next-step conversations and boundaries

It excludes:

- therapy
- crisis support
- compatibility scoring
- attachment-style diagnosis
- a universal map of all human bonds

## Primary Users

### Primary

- adults navigating friendship that may deepen
- adults navigating early romance without wanting to drift into ambiguity

### Secondary

- community builders and collaborators navigating trust and role clarity

## Jobs To Be Done

- Help me describe this relationship without fantasy or minimization.
- Help me decide whether I want it to deepen at all.
- Help me see what the next layer would require from both sides.
- Help me hold a boundary without becoming avoidant or vague.
- Help me spot asymmetry before I overinvest.

## Core Product Model

The product answers four questions:

1. What layer are we actually in?
2. What direction do I want?
3. Are reciprocity, safety, pace, and repair strong enough for that move?
4. What is the clean next action?

## Initial Layer Framework

- Contact: recognition without tested trust
- Warmth: positive contact and low-stakes consistency
- Trust: disclosure and follow-through are becoming real
- Reliance: mutual dependence is present and acknowledged
- Intimacy or kinship: identity-level closeness and durable inclusion

These layers describe the bond as it exists. Progression should never be the default assumption.

## MVP Scope

### Inputs

- relationship context
- current layer
- desired direction
- readiness signals such as reciprocity, safety, pace alignment, repair capacity, and boundary clarity

### Outputs

- recommended direction: deepen, hold, clarify, or step back
- short rationale
- one concrete conversation prompt
- one suggested practice or boundary
- one green flag to watch for
- one stopping condition

## Non-Goals For v1

- full team dynamics tooling
- social graph mapping
- partner matching
- trauma interpretation
- mental health intervention
- legal, medical, or crisis advice

## Safety Design Requirements

- No diagnosis of the other person.
- No certainty language about motives or outcomes.
- No pressure toward vulnerability for its own sake.
- A “do not deepen” outcome must be treated as valid and healthy.
- The product must include a clear note about scope and crisis limitations.

## Utility Standard

The output should change what the user says or does next.

Every session should end with:

- a question to ask
- a boundary to name
- a pacing experiment
- a reason to pause

## Product Risks

### Risk: It becomes reflective wallpaper

Mitigation:
- insist on behavioral outputs
- avoid poetic abstraction unless tied to an action

### Risk: It rewards projection

Mitigation:
- center observable behavior
- avoid claims about hidden motives

### Risk: It becomes emotionally overreaching

Mitigation:
- keep scope narrow
- use plain language
- warn against using the tool as proof in conflict

## Success Criteria

- Users report clearer next-step conversations.
- Users can distinguish desire from inertia.
- Users can identify when holding or stepping back is healthier than deepening.
- The product feels humane without feeling clinical or manipulative.

## Build Recommendation

Build a bounded prototype with:

- friendship and early-romance focused
- consent-forward
- explicitly non-therapeutic
- action-oriented rather than mystical

Expansion depends on usage. If people use it for clarity, continue. If they use it to justify fantasies or certainty, tighten the model first.
